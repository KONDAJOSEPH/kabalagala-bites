Delivery, payment and hours

Delivery

We deliver around Kabalagala, Muyenga, Ggaba Road and Kansanga. The delivery fee depends on distance and is confirmed on WhatsApp before we leave.

Questions people ask

How do I order?

Tap Order now on any dish, or add several dishes to your order and send them together on WhatsApp.

How long does food take?

Most orders are ready in about 15 minutes. Delivery time depends on how far you are.

Do you deliver?

Yes, inside Kabalagala and nearby areas. The fee is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can I pay with Mobile Money?

Yes. We accept cash, MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money.

Do you cook for parties and offices?

Yes. Message us the number of people and the date and we will send you a price.
